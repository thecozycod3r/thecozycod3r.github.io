Websites

Five sites for real businesses. Each page explains the design language of one site and the reasoning behind how it looks and works.

  • Heritage members’ club, Yercaud, Tamil Nadu

    Yercaud Club

    A hill club founded in 1897, with no website of its own. Rebuilt from a tariff card, a souvenir booklet and the club’s own photographs into a four-page site for members, affiliates and guests, now live on the club’s own domain.

    Cutive and Manrope

  • Coffee estate, green coffee producer, Chikmagalur, Karnataka

    Nandipura & Indravally Estates

    A fifth-generation estate growing coffee under native shade since 1900. The site positions them as what they are — a producer selling green coffee to roasters — not another café brand.

    Libre Caslon Display, Libre Caslon Text and Libre Franklin

  • Private pool villa, Sakleshpur, Karnataka

    Bhramari

    A one-acre private pool villa inside a coffee estate. One page that sells a weekend: open the gates, walk the path, check a date, message the owner.

    Sacramento, Cormorant Garamond and Jost

  • Creative studios + café, Bengaluru

    Anti Labs Studios

    A photo studio, a dance studio and a café under one roof. A pixel-accurate rebuild of their Squarespace site, plus two new pages designed in the same language.

    Boldonse, Space Mono and Space Grotesk

  • Curated luxury travel, India

    Unravel Experiences

    A travel company that sells short trips built around one remarkable stay. With the Unravel team I rebuilt the site so each trip, called an Original, reads like an itinerary with a price, and added a members’ area for Unravel Access.

    Plus Jakarta Sans

How I approach a site

Identity comes from the place

Colours are sampled from the crest, the canopy, the brick path in the hero shot. Nothing is a template palette.

Only facts the client can stand behind

Rates, dates, certifications and reviews come from the client’s own documents. Unknowns stay off the page.

Built for the phone they will use

Static, prerendered pages, compressed images and motion that steps aside on slow connections or reduced-motion settings.

One clear next step

Each site ends in the action the business actually takes: a WhatsApp message, an enquiry, a call.
