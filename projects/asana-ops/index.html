Asana Operations OverhaulAsana Operations Overhaul

Turned a siloed, deadline-missing agency team into a coordinated operation — by implementing Asana company-wide with custom automation, workload visibility, and an integrated leave management system.

OperationsAutomationProject Management

The problem

Four teams, zero coordination.

When I audited DAM's operations, every function was running in isolation. The creatives didn't know what Google Ads needed. Account managers didn't know what the designers were working on. Deadlines were tracked in personal to-do apps, WhatsApp chats, and memory. Requirements were being missed. People were overloaded in silence while others had capacity.

There was no single place to see who was working on what, when things were due, or how loaded each person was. Management was reactive — firefighting instead of planning.

Siloed teams

Creatives, Ads, Account Mgmt — no cross-team visibility

Missed requirements

Tasks falling through the cracks between handoffs

Unbalanced workloads

No way to see who was overloaded or underutilised

No leave visibility

Leave tracked via WhatsApp, not tied to project planning

The build

Asana as the operating system.

I implemented Asana as the company-wide project management platform — not just as a task tracker, but as the operational nervous system of the business. Custom automation, two workload dashboards, and an integrated leave management system were built on top.

Features

What was built on top of Asana.

Smart Auto-Assignment

Custom rules auto-assign tasks to the right person based on project type and role — eliminating the need for managers to manually distribute work on every brief.

Dual Workload Dashboards

Two dashboards built: one showing tasks by project (project workload view), one showing tasks by person (assignee workload view). Managers can rebalance in real time.

Slack + Email Notifications

Automated notifications triggered by task status changes — team members know immediately when something lands in their queue or moves to the next stage.

Zapier Leave Tracker

Employees submit leave via a Zapier-triggered form. Manager approves in one click. Approval triggers an email notification and the leave appears in a shared calendar — tied to project planning.

Behind the scenes — actual Zapier wiring

One trigger, four conditional paths. Each leave type subtracts the correct number of days from the right balance and writes back to Asana — no manual reconciliation.

Tech stack

No-code automation on top of Asana.

AsanaZapierSlackAsana Rules (native automation)Email (SMTP via Zapier)Google Calendar

The entire system was built without writing a single line of code. Asana's native rules engine handled task auto-assignment and status-triggered notifications. Zapier connected the leave form to Asana, Slack, and email. The workload dashboards were built using Asana's reporting tools with custom filters.
