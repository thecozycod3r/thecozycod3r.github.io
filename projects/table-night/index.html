Table Night

Fourteen multiplayer card and party games for a room full of friends. Everyone plays on their own phone, no accounts, no app store, free. Open a table, share the code, deal.

The problem

Game night, minus the box.

It started with Wizard, a card game my friends play, and a paper scorebook nobody wanted to keep. Then the box was not always there and people joined late. I wanted a table that lives on everyone's phones and survives all of that.

The games

Fourteen tables, one shared engine.

Wizard, Splendor, Seven Marvels, Avalon, Incan Gold, UNO Flip, UNO No Mercy, 6 Nimmt!, Skull King, Coup, Spyfall, Codenames, Kings Cup and Ride the Bus. Each has its own rules engine, its own art and a Rules button on every screen; the lobby, seats, rejoin, timers and podium are shared.

📱

Your Phone Is Your Hand

Private cards on your screen, the shared table on everyone's. Works on any phone browser, sideways or upright.

🔁

No Host to Lose

One phone runs the table and broadcasts state. If it goes quiet for seven seconds, the next seat takes over; when it comes back it steps aside.

🙋

Rejoin From Anywhere

Seats are remembered on the device. A new phone can claim its seat with ‘Which player are you?’ and carry on mid-round.

🎨

Original Art

Every game has its own illustrated cards and posters, generated and art-directed per game rather than copied from the boxes.

Judgement calls

Tested like it matters.

  • Every engine is simulated over thousands of bot games before it ships, to catch deadlocks and rule edge cases.
  • Real-device testing: headless runs at four and six players, host-failover tests, slow-network and phone-sleep tests, and a pass on a real iPhone.
  • No backend of my own. A realtime channel per table carries the state, so there is nothing to keep running and nothing to pay for.
